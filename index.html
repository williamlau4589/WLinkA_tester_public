WorkplanLink reads a PDF drawing set the way a careful colleague would: it fixes the scale from your own dimensions, cross-checks the disciplines, counts the quantities, and hands back a plain register of what to look at. How it works

Three steps, and nothing to install

01 Sign in with email Tap the button, sign in with your email, and tell us your firm and project in one short form.
02 Upload & pick outputs Drop in your PDF set — architectural, and structural or M&E if you have them — and choose what you'd like back. If the project is sensitive, use project codes rather than real names.
03 Get your results We run the set and send back your schedules and a flag register, as soon as possible, with a note on what to trust.

Your drawings, your call

How we treat your set

  • Confidentiality honoured. We comply with your project's confidentiality terms. To make that easy, you're welcome to use project codes and initials in place of client and personal names before you upload — the checks work just as well on a coded set.
  • Used only for your test. We run your set to produce what you asked for, and for nothing else.
  • Not shared. Your drawings aren't passed to third parties or shown to other testers.
  • Deleted on request. Tell us when you're done and we remove the files.
  • Coordination, not certification. Design adequacy stays with the engineer of record and the QP. WorkplanLink checks whether the set agrees with itself.
